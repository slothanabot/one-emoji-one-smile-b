One emoji, one smile: 🙃 — the upside-down face. A tiny, deterministic Python program that writes exactly that one emoji to the output file, nothing else.

## Why the upside-down face makes people smile

The upside-down face is a smile with a wink of mischief. It is the face of someone who just said something silly on purpose and knows it — playful, not pushy, and instantly readable in any language. A stranger reading it does not need context, a shared joke, or a second look: the smile is right there, just tilted, and the tilt is the joke. It is warm without being intimate, funny without being sarcastic, and familiar without being the default smiley everyone reaches for first. It says "I am smiling at you, and I am in on the joke with you" — the kind of small, unexpected warmth that makes a stranger smile back.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes exactly one emoji to `out/entry.txt`.
